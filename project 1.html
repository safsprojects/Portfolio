Organizing the 1st ever B.I. study trip!

Description:As part of the first-ever Business Innovation Study Trip, I was selected to join the organizing team, giving me the chance to work closely with lecturers, business partners, and fellow students to create an immersive international experience. My role involved coordinating logistics, planning the schedule, and aligning learning objectives with hands-on company visits. I contributed to designing sessions that blended innovation-focused lectures, workshops, and company tours, ensuring participants gained both theoretical and practical insights. This project was particularly exciting because it pioneered a new type of study trip, setting a standard for future international business experiences. I developed strong project management skills by juggling timelines, resources, and stakeholder expectations; cross-cultural communication skills through interactions with international partners; and problem-solving abilities when unexpected challenges arose during planning and execution. Beyond the organizational side, the experience gave me a firsthand look at how businesses innovate across borders, enriching my understanding of international business practices.

Technologies Used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript (replace with your tech stack)

Role: As one of the organizers, and therefore one of the responsible people during the whole project, I was contantly working towards and during the execution of trip. Apart from the stakeholder engagements I was the one responsible for the customer engagement between us as the organizers and the first year BI students to come on the trip. The trip was a non-mandatory part of the their study, however provided a unique academic development opportunity. It was part of my role to communicate this with our customers in terms of marketing to sell our trip for them to want to join. I covered several aspects of bonding, informing, updating, and all visuals relating to the trip and execution (such as a logo, slogan, color theme, typograhpy, presentaitons, and overall allignment).
